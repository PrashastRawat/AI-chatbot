A quiet companion for the questions
that sit heavy

Bring a worry, a decision, or a day that feels hard to carry. What follows draws loosely on the Bhagavad Gita — one voice among many, not the final word.

This offers reflection, not therapy, medical, legal, or crisis advice, and doesn't ask anyone to adopt a belief. If you're in danger or thinking about harming yourself, please contact local emergency services or someone you trust right now.
